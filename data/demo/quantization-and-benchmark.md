INT8 quantization maps fp32 weights to 8-bit integers, making the ONNX file roughly a quarter of its original size with little retrieval quality loss. FP16 stores weights in 16-bit floats and halves the size. Compression ratio is measured as original size divided by quantized size, and the quality change must be reported with Recall@K and nDCG@K on the same evaluation set before and after quantization.
